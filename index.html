Prologue · Ota, Nigeria · 2018

It started with numbers.

I studied Industrial Mathematics at Covenant University: the craft of turning messy real world problems into models you can act on. My thesis looked at how market research shapes fintech products.

In the evenings I designed for Hebron Startup Lab and TEDxCovenantUniversity. Somewhere between the equations and the posters, I found product design, the place where numbers meet people.
